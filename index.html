Bio

My research interests include advanced machine learning, pattern recognition, and signal processing. I particularly focus on developing computer vision models for the effective extraction and interpretation of complex, high-dimensional data. I am also motivated by challenges in limited-data and low-resource environments and aim to design efficient and generalizable algorithms for real-world applications.
